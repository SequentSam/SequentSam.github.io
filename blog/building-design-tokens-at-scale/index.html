Building Design Tokens at Scale

A phased approach to rolling out design tokens across multiple products without breaking existing UI.

Design tokens are only as helpful as the pipeline that distributes them. I recommend starting with color and type styles, then expanding into spacing and component-specific tokens once the team is comfortable with the workflow. In this post I outline how to sync tokens between Figma, GitHub, and Eleventy builds with minimal human intervention.

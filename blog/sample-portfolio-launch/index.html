Ship a Portfolio in a Weekend

A lightweight playbook for standing up an Eleventy portfolio, from content outline to a polished deploy on GitHub Pages.

If you keep putting off your personal site because it feels like a big lift, this weekend-sized plan will get you live. The goal is momentum over perfection: pick a simple stack, write only what matters, and ship on Sunday night.

Saturday: structure and content

  • Outline the pages. Home, About, Contact, and a Blog landing page are enough. Give yourself one featured project and one story or article.
  • Write fast. Draft bullets before prose. Aim for ~150 words per page and a 3–4 sentence hero story that says who you are, what you do, and who you help.
  • Collect assets. One portrait, one project screenshot, and a simple favicon are enough. Keep filenames short and lowercase.

Saturday evening: assemble in Eleventy

  • Clone a starter or scaffold with npx @11ty/eleventy --init. Keep the folder structure clean: src/, _includes/, _data/.
  • Wire your content into templates. Use a single layout for pages and a blog layout for posts to keep styling consistent.
  • Add basic SEO. Set <title>, meta description, and social share image in a shared head partial.

Sunday: polish and deploy

  • Apply a palette and type pair (one sans, one display). Keep buttons and links on-brand.
  • Test the responsive layout. Check nav, hero, cards, and forms at 360px, 768px, and desktop widths.
  • Deploy to GitHub Pages. Push to main, let your workflow publish _site, and verify your CNAME points to your custom domain.

After launch: iterate in small steps

Ship small improvements weekly: add a case study, a new article, or a signup form. Once the foundation is live, it’s easier to keep showing up. The important part is that your work is visible and current.***

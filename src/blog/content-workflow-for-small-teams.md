When resources are tight, process must be simple. My preferred workflow uses three stages: idea, draft, and live. Each stage maps to a Git branch with Eleventy preview URLs so stakeholders can review real pages instead of documents. Templates handle the structure, so the team can focus on copy.

We also lean on shared image assets and short content briefs that capture the purpose of every article. Automations take care of resizing hero images and posting teasers to social, leaving the team time to interview customers and refine messaging.

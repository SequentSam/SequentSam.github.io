Accessibility work does not happen all at once; it is layered into every sprint. My audit checklist pairs human review with a healthy dose of automation. Start with semantic HTML, then walk a screen reader through critical flows. Follow up with automated color contrast checks and keyboard traps.

Most importantly, capture the gaps in a living doc. **Teams** that track accessibility issues alongside their feature work close them significantly faster than those who treat accessibility like a quarterly project. If you need a starting point, download the checklist template linked at the end of this article and adapt it to your stack.

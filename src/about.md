## About the project

This starter gives you a clean Eleventy setup with sensible defaults. It is intentionally small so you can understand every piece and tailor it to your workflow.

### Tech stack

- **Eleventy** for the static build pipeline
- **Nunjucks** templates for layouts
- Plain CSS for styling (swap in your favorite framework)

Feel free to delete this page once you have your own content ready.
